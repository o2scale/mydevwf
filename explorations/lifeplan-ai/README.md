**Vision:** Copy-tradeable life workflows powered by AI across all your devices

## What is This?

This folder contains the vision, analysis, and future development of **LifePlan AI** - a Personal AI Operating System that extends beyond productivity apps into a comprehensive life management ecosystem.

**Core Concept:** Just like copy-trading in finance, people can import and adapt proven "life workflows" - with AI personalizing execution to their unique context.

---

## Key Innovation

**Copy-Tradeable Life Workflows** = Notion templates × Zapier automation × AI coaching

- Import proven workflows (Startup Founder, Developer, Student, Parent, etc.)
- AI adapts to your context
- Track results over time
- Network effects: "10,000 people using the 'Startup Founder' workflow"
